How it works

  1. Attach a Drive folder and enable Away mode above.
  2. Every hour, new PDFs in the folder are reviewed automatically.
  3. Each file gets a CSV report in the Reports subfolder, then moves to Processed (clean) or Needs attention (issues found).
  4. Files the reviewer doesn't recognize are left alone.
